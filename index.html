Circular Economy

The 450-Year Plastic Timeline

A single PET plastic bottle takes between 450 to 500 years to decompose in nature. In sunlight, it fragments into microscopic particles (microplastics) that contaminate water and soil. Segregating into the Blue Bin ensures it is mechanically shredded into fresh polyester fibers instead!

🌿 Friendly Cleanliness & Segregation Habits

💧

The 10-Second Quick Rinse

Rinsing drink cartons and food containers prevents mold and protects dry cardboard from contamination in municipal collection trucks.

✂️

Keep Packet Corners Attached

When snipping open milk and oil pouches, make a small slit without detaching the small corner tip to avoid creating loose micro-plastic debris.

🪴

Home Compost Wet Scraps

Fruit and vegetable scraps break down into nitrogen-rich plant food in 30 days, keeping methane-producing organic waste completely out of landfills.
